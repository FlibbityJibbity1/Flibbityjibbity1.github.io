How to Avoid Phishing and Getting Hacked

NEVER click suspious download links.

They could contain malicious files that may compromise your information!

Top 5 Tips to Stay Safe Online

  • Verify the Sender's Email: Always look at the actual email address, not just the display name. Scammers often use domains that look slightly off (e.g., security@paypaI.com instead of paypal.com).
  • Hover Before You Click: Hover your mouse cursor over any links in an email to see the actual destination URL at the bottom of your browser before you click on it.
  • Enable Two-Factor Authentication (2FA): Always use 2FA on your important accounts. Even if a hacker steals your password, they won't be able to log in without the second code from your phone or authenticator app.
  • Beware of Urgency and Threats: Phishing emails often try to induce panic (e.g., "Your account will be suspended in 24 hours!"). Take a breath, do not click the link, and log in to the service manually through your browser to check.
  • Watch Out for Unexpected Downloads: Never run or open files you weren't expecting to receive. Notice how easily a file just downloaded to your machine? If that was an executable (.exe) instead of a text file, clicking it could compromise your system.
