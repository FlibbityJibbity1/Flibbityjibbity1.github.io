I am a New York-based vocalist, actor, and musician (ukuleleist really, but that sounds less cool) who got my start at age 4 tagging along with my parents singing at local karaoke bars, and working as a background actor on film & TV from age 3.

Since then, I have performed as part of New York City Center's revivals of Into the Woods & Lionel Bart's Oliver! (ensemble), sung at Carnegie Hall twice with the All-City High School Chorus, and won first place (and second the year before) for illustration at Baruch College's Excellence in high school journalism awards.

Recently, I have begun to branch out into computer science (the arts are not particularly stable career choices), and am currently studying cybersecurity at NYU Tandon. Aside from that, I enjoy homelabbing, learning guitar (very badly), taking long walks on the beach at sunset, and restoring old computers I find in the trash or at flea markets.
